## Deliberate boundaries and open question

The new worker tool is link_stripe_customer. Designer discovery/calls are
blocked explicitly. The existing MCP authorization userId api-service has
admin access, without super_admin privileges. It therefore receives 403 from
this super-admin-only route, even with a full-profile recorded actor. The
route is usable through a signed-in super-admin browser session. No recorded
actor is ever passed into an authorization check. Making the action usable
through MCP requires a separately approved authorization design; it remains
an open question, rather than an elevation introduced by this batch.
