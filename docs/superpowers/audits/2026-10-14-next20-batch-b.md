## Item 11, LW.42

Every Airwallex sync automatically reads the active USD and AUD BANK accounts
whose normalised names end in the matching YIELD_USD or YIELD_AUD suffix,
through the existing Xero client. CurrencyCode must match, and exactly one
account must match each currency. The optional finance.yieldAccountIds setting
can pin either currency to an AccountID, still requiring ACTIVE BANK status
and the matching currency. Invalid or unresolved pins fail closed.
Both accounts, a unique closing
balance column, and the organisation's base currency must be readable.
Missing, ambiguous, invalid, failed or timed-out reads retain the saved holdings
and their freshness. The entire Xero read has a 15-second deadline, including
token acquisition and response-body waits.

Xero BankSummary reports base-currency balances. The saved entries therefore
keep the report's currency and exact amount, plus sourceAccountId and
sourceAccountCurrency. An NZD report amount is never relabelled USD/AUD or
reverse-converted using an estimated exchange rate. Materialised yield:NZD
can replace old yield:USD/AUD rows. That replacement and the settings timestamp
are one D1 batch transaction, so a failed write cannot remove cash or stamp an
unmaterialised balance as freshly read. Malformed saved amounts such as null,
blank strings, booleans or arrays leave existing yield rows untouched.

Only documented yield accounts may use Xero as their source. Removed the
Xero wallet fallback from the shared cash position, financial summary,
bank-balances response and forward snapshot metrics. Airwallex wallet and
materialised yield rows now form the cash basis across all four readers.
Forward metrics return unknown cash when no Airwallex rows exist.

Yield freshness comes from finance.yieldHoldings.updatedAt, never the wallet
sync time. Older than 30 days, unknown or future timestamps produce a visible
warning that cash may be out of date on the home Cash runway card and finance
Bank balances card. Bank sync freshness ignores yield rows. The worker's new
bounded sync_airwallex tool dispatches to the same route, with no caller-supplied
transaction-window argument. It is blocked for the designer profile;
get_bank_balances includes the same freshness metadata.

The finance bank table labels amounts as Reported and explains account versus
yield report currency. Its forex card is explicitly wallet-only: it excludes
base-valued yield rows, and computes shares after conversion into NZD instead
of summing unlike native units. A zero-funded wallet has no exposure card.
This prevents an NZD valuation of foreign yield from being presented as a real
NZD holding. lib/wallet-forex.ts and its currency/empty regressions cover this.
