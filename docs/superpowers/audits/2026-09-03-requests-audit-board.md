## Nothing lost

Confirmed present and faithful to the prototype:

- **Proxy scrollbar.** Full parity in `board-scrollbar.tsx`: 0.75rem track, 44px minimum thumb (`MIN_THUMB_PX`), pointer drag covering mouse, pen and touch, click-to-page on the bare track, 1.75rem arrows paging exactly one column with smooth behaviour, Arrow keys plus Home and End, `role="scrollbar"` with `aria-controls` and a live `aria-valuenow`, hidden when nothing overflows, re-measured on a card-count signature so adding or moving a card updates it, and 2.75rem arrows on mobile.
- **Kanban geometry.** Columns fixed at 16.5rem (264px) with a 0.875rem (14px) gap in one non-wrapping row; a column never scrolls and never gains a max height; the scroller owns the horizontal scroll and the page owns the vertical; `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory` with `scroll-snap-align: start` below 47.9375rem.
- **Card hover.** 1px lift with `var(--shadow-sm)`, 150ms symmetric, disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Drag state rotates the card by -1.5deg.
- **People stack.** Client avatar on the left for admins only, delivery people stacked right with -0.4375rem overlap and a 2px background ring, three visible then a "+N" chip, a dashed unassigned dot, tooltips naming person and role, and every dot focusable with `tabIndex={0}` so the tooltip is reachable by keyboard as well as hover.
- **Subtask preview.** Bar with progress, count label and a chevron that rotates on expand, inline list with status dot, strikethrough on delivered rows, title and assignee avatar with a dashed slot for unassigned, lazy fetched through SWR and cached per URL so re-expanding is instant, open state keyed by id so a status move or filter change does not collapse it, and `onDragStart` suppressed on the bar so pressing it never drags the card.
- **Card rules.** Delivered cards hide the due chip while keeping the date for the timeline; the title clamps to two lines with `overflow-wrap: break-word`; the reference pins to the top right; cards are keyboard operable with Enter and Space, which the prototype did not offer.
- **Timeline.** Every repo feature kept: per-column legend plus Overdue, Today button with calendar icon, auto-scroll placing today 30 percent into the plotted area measured past the sticky label column, bars for items with a start date and milestone diamonds otherwise, overdue red with a darker ring, sticky label column, and edge-extension buffers that make the chart feel endless. Prototype additions landed: 16.25rem (260px) label column with reference, title and client avatar, 10.625rem (170px) with two-line titles on mobile, rows keyboard focusable and clickable, zebra striping the sticky cell inherits, week guides painted as a repeating gradient locked to today, and delivered never overdue. Domain maths is extracted to `lib/timeline-domain.ts` with tests.
- **Capacity strip.** One lane per track, live tile with dot, title, assignee avatar and first name, due chip and progress with percent, dashed empty-lane placeholder reading "Open. Next in queue pulls in.", numbered queue with a "Next up" pill on the first row. Wired to the real `/api/portal/capacity` and to per-request steps for honest progress rather than the prototype's seeded numbers, with a status-based fallback. Queue rows are 2.75rem buttons, better than the prototype's plain rows. Renders nothing for a client with no subscription or no tracks.
- **View routing.** Four peer views with the correct icons, Workload admin-only, roving tabindex with arrow keys and Home and End, icon-only below 1024px with `title` and `aria-label`, 2.75rem targets on mobile.
- **No duplicate queue view.** `TrackQueueView` is not mounted on the Requests page. It appears only in `app/(dashboard)/tracks/tracks-content.tsx:96` and `app/(dashboard)/clients/[id]/client-detail.tsx:2756`.

## Open questions for the lead

1. **On-hold and cancelled requests vanish from the board.** `BOARD_COLS` at `request-list.tsx:131` has five columns, matching the prototype's `PIPELINE`, but `ALL_STATUSES` and the project guide both list On Hold and Cancelled. A request in either status is grouped under a status with no column and is silently not rendered. Prototype parity is fine here; product correctness may not be. Add the columns, or add a rail affordance that says how many requests are off-board?
2. **The capacity strip is not live for clients.** It mounts under `railOn && !isAdmin`, and `railOn` is `isSuperAdmin`, so only Liam or Staci impersonating a client ever sees it. Intentional per the code comment. When does it open up?
3. **Workload was never in the port plan.** Slices 4, 5 and 7 cover kanban, timeline and capacity; Workload only gained a route. W1 and W2 are correctness bugs worth fixing regardless, but W3 is a build. In or out?
4. **`/tracks` versus the capacity strip.** Both explain retainer capacity to a client, in different visual languages. Is `/tracks` staying in the client navigation once the strip lands?
5. **Dark mode shadows.** `--shadow-sm` resolves to `none` in dark mode (`globals.css:1367`), so the card hover lift in dark mode is carried by the border colour and the 1px transform alone. Acceptable, or does the board want its own hover shadow?
