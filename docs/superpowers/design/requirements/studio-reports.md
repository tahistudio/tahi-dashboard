# Design requirements: studio-reports

Group: studio-reports (audience: studio only). Routes: `/reports`.
Prepared 2026-09-13 for the Claude Design pass. Source: CLAUDE.md, STATUS.md, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-13-page-catalogue.md`, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-13-design-review-checklist.md`, TASKS.md, live code (`app/(dashboard)/reports/page.tsx`, `app/(dashboard)/reports/reports-content.tsx`, the 18 routes under `app/api/admin/reports`, `lib/permissions.ts`, `lib/feature-tree.ts`, `lib/access-scoping.ts`, `lib/require-access.ts`, `lib/require-feature.ts`), and the Claude Design project (57bf60cf-5e6d-450f-9e2f-e25c8d12fd66), which holds no file for this surface at all.

## 1. Purpose and audiences

`/reports` is the studio's "broad analytics hub": one long scrolling page, ten sections under a sticky jump nav, answering operations, sales-pipeline and finance-shape questions in one place. It exists beside two other, more specific pages that ask the same questions in more depth: `/sales-analytics` (pipeline shape) and `/financial-reports` (the daily-trusted finance cockpit). `/reports` was never triaged after those two were built, so today it is mostly a duplicate: six of its ten sections restate `/sales-analytics` and five restate `/financial-reports`, plus a second, diverged editor for the same fixed-cost commitments that `/financial-reports` also edits (`app/(dashboard)/reports/reports-content.tsx:3017`, TASKS.md CT.19).

Audiences:
- **Liam and Staci (super admin / owner).** Full access to every section, every client's numbers, unconditionally.
- **A scoped Tahi team member granted the `reports` feature.** Seed 0041 grants `reports.view` to the `project_manager` and `viewer` roles by default, so a first studio hire can reach this page without an explicit override. `reports` is deliberately kept as its own permission resource, separate from `financial_reports` (cash, MRR, runway, reserves are studio-private and unseeded, per the comment at `lib/permissions.ts:82-87`), specifically so a project manager cannot see the money screens just by holding the operational-reports grant.
- **Nobody else.** `lib/feature-tree.ts` registers `reports` with `appliesTo: ['team']` only. There is no client variant and no portal equivalent. `app/(dashboard)/reports/page.tsx` redirects to `/requests` whenever `isAdmin` is false or `isPreviewingClient` is true, with the same comment pattern used on `/calls` and `/deals`: a studio preview of "what a client sees" must never leak one client's numbers into another's view.

What it must never show:
- A client org's numbers to a team member outside their `teamMemberAccess` scope. **This is currently broken.** The front end renders all ten sections unconditionally with no permission-aware hiding at all, and most of the API routes behind those sections enforce a *feature* gate (`reports`, `financial_reports`, or `sales_analytics` via `requireFeature`) but do not call `resolveAccessScoping` / `getOrgScope` to restrict the underlying rows to the caller's assigned clients. Concretely: `overview`, `response-time`, `sales`, `close-rates`, `financial-trends`, `cash-flow-forecast`, `invoice-aging`, `bank-balances`, `expenses`, and `utilization` all import `requireFeature` but never `access-scoping` or `require-access`, so a scoped hire who is granted `reports` (the default) plus any explicit `financial_reports` or `sales_analytics` allow would see studio-wide MRR, cash, and AR aging, not just their own clients'. Only `retainer-health` and `client-profitability` (both import `getOrgScope`) and `worklog` (imports `resolveAccessScoping`) scope correctly today. This is the exact gap the catalogue names: "no access-scoping helper is called so a scoped hire sees studio-wide MRR and aging."
- Studio-private finance data (cash position, bank balances, MRR, AR aging, take-home, client margin) to anyone who holds only the operational `reports` resource and not `financial_reports`. The API layer does enforce this split correctly per-route (`sales`, `financial-trends`, `cash-flow-forecast`, `invoice-aging`, `bank-balances`, `client-profitability`, `expenses` all gate on `financial_reports`; `close-rates` gates on `sales_analytics`; only `overview`, `response-time`, `retainer-health`, `utilization`, `worklog` gate on plain `reports`), but the page itself never reads the caller's resolved access and never hides the Finance or Sales jump-nav groups or their anchors. A team member who holds `reports` but not `financial_reports` therefore lands on a page that visibly advertises five Finance sections and a Sales section they cannot actually load; each one 403s silently into a stuck skeleton or an empty chart rather than being hidden. See section 4.
- Anything this page's own commitments editor and the `/financial-reports` commitments editor disagree about. Both write to the same `/api/admin/commitments` endpoint from two separately-maintained pieces of UI, and per the catalogue they have already diverged.
- No super-admin-only sub-area exists inside `/reports` beyond the ordinary admin/scoped-member split described above.

## 2. Pages, sub pages and entry points

- **`/reports`** (the only route). Reached from the sidebar "Team/Ops" or reports-adjacent nav group, and by deep link. Server-gated by `requirePageFeature('reports')` after the admin/preview checks in `page.tsx`. There is no separate detail, slide-over, or dialog anywhere on this surface: it is a single long page.
- **Sticky jump nav (`<SectionTabs items={REPORTS_SECTIONS} />`).** Not a route change and not a tab switch in the usual sense: clicking an item scrolls the page to an anchor `<div id="...">` (`overview`, `financial-health`, `response-time`, `sales-pipeline`, `retainer-health`, `commitments`, `cash-flow`, `expenses`, `client-profitability`, `utilization`). Every section is always mounted and always fetching; the jump nav only changes scroll position. `REPORTS_SECTIONS` also carries a `group` field (`Operations` | `Sales` | `Finance` | `Team`) used purely to visually cluster the jump-nav items, not to gate anything.
- **Currency selector**, nested in the sticky jump-nav row, drives `useDisplayCurrency()` and reformats every money figure on the page (not a separate view; a global display preference).
- **Ten always-rendered sections**, each its own component, no lazy mount and no per-section loading gate independent of its own SWR call:
  1. **Overview** (`id="overview"`), KPI strip (Total Clients, Open Requests, Billable Hours, Outstanding) plus a Requests-by-Status donut and a Monthly Request Volume bar chart, from `GET /api/admin/reports/overview`.
  2. **Financial Health** (`id="financial-health"`, `FinancialHealthSection`), duplicates `/financial-reports`.
  3. **Response Time** (`id="response-time"`; note the page's DOM ids and the `REPORTS_SECTIONS` list do not fully line up 1:1 with render order, so treat the ten `REPORTS_SECTIONS` entries as the canonical section list a design should honour), per-team-member average first-response time, from `GET /api/admin/reports/response-time`, with a CSV export ("Export CSV" button, downloads `response-times.csv` client-side, no server round trip).
  4. **Pipeline** (`sales-pipeline`, `SalesPipelineSection`), composes `SalesFunnelSection`, `SourceBreakdownSection`, `StageVelocitySection`, `CloseRateSourceBreakdownSection`, and `SalesCycleLengthSection`: five sub-sections drawing on `GET /api/admin/reports/sales` and `GET /api/admin/reports/close-rates`. This is the "six sections duplicate `/sales-analytics`" cluster the catalogue names (the parent plus five children).
  5. **Retainer Health** (`retainer-health`, `RetainerHealthSection`), per-retainer-client health and churn-risk scoring, from `GET /api/admin/reports/retainer-health` (the one Sales-group route that does call `getOrgScope`).
  6. **Fixed Costs** (`commitments`, `CommitmentsSection`), a full CRUD editor (add, edit, deactivate) for `commitments` rows, feeding the cash-flow forecast. This is the second, diverged commitments editor CT.19 flags; `/financial-reports` has its own.
  7. **Cash Flow** (`cash-flow`, `CashFlowForecastSection`), 6 to 12 month projection, from `GET /api/admin/reports/cash-flow-forecast`.
  8. **Xero P&L (past)** (`expenses`, `ExpenseDashboardSection`), categorised historical expenses and P&L trend, from `GET /api/admin/reports/expenses` and `/expenses/category`.
  9. **Client Margin** (`client-profitability`, `ClientProfitabilityScorecard`), per-client gross margin scorecard, from `GET /api/admin/reports/client-profitability` (the other Finance-group route that scopes correctly).
  10. **Team Utilisation** (`utilization`, `UtilizationSection`), per-team-member billable-vs-available hours, from `GET /api/admin/reports/utilization`.
- The Xero P&L section also carries a 3/6/12/24-month range picker (`windowMonths`, cached per range via the SWR key) and two write actions, "Sync P&L" and "Sync Bank Balances", each `POST`ing to `/api/admin/integrations/xero/sync-pnl` and `/api/admin/integrations/xero/sync-balances` respectively and showing an inline `aria-live` status line ("Pulling 12 months of P&L from Xero...", "Synced N month(s)", "Sync failed. Check Xero connection.", "Sync error") for about four seconds. This is a second write surface beyond Fixed Costs; see section 4 for the gate mismatch it introduces.
- No dialogs. Two sections carry inline write actions: Fixed Costs (add/edit/deactivate a commitment) and Xero P&L (the two sync buttons above); every other section is read-only charts and tables.

## 3. States and variants

- **Loading (page-level, before the Overview KPI fetch resolves).** A bespoke skeleton with a raw `<h1>Reports</h1>` and paragraph (not `<PageHeader>`) plus four pulsing KPI-shaped boxes. No `<SectionTabs>` renders during this state, so the jump nav itself pops in only once the first fetch resolves. This is what the catalogue means by "Partial (PageHeader only)": `PageHeader` is used, but only in the loaded branch, never in the loading branch.
- **Loading (per-section, after page-level load).** Each of the ten sections independently shows its own skeleton while its own SWR call is in flight: `animate-pulse` bar rows in several sections, `SkeletonChart`, `SkeletonTable`, or `SkeletonProgressList` in others. No shared skeleton language across all ten; three different skeleton primitives are in use plus bespoke `animate-pulse` divs.
- **Empty (a section with genuinely zero rows).** Inline text only, no `EmptyState` component anywhere on this page (`app/(dashboard)/reports/reports-content.tsx` has zero `EmptyState` references). Copy varies by section; the Overview charts use "Data will appear once you have requests and clients." for both the status donut and the monthly trend when their arrays are empty. Other sections either render an empty chart/table with no explanatory copy at all, or (proposal) need one written.
- **Error.** Only the page-level Overview fetch has an explicit error affordance: `error` (from `overviewErr`) plus a `fetchData`/`mutate` retry. The rendered UI is a leaf-radius brand-gradient icon badge, an "Unable to load reports" heading, one line of copy, and a brand-filled "Retry" button (`reports-content.tsx:135-170`), reusing the same visual language as the empty-state pattern elsewhere in the app even though this page has no `EmptyState` component calls. None of the other nine sections were confirmed to have a distinct error state versus silently showing an empty chart when their own SWR call fails or 403s; treat every section's error state as unverified and needing an explicit design (see section 7).
- **403 from an unmet feature gate (Finance and Sales sections, member holding only `reports`).** Not a designed state at all today. A team member who can open `/reports` (via the `reports` feature) but lacks `financial_reports` will have five sections' fetches (`sales`, `financial-trends`, `cash-flow-forecast`, `invoice-aging`..., `invoice-aging` is not rendered on this page today, see note below, `bank-balances` likewise not rendered here, `client-profitability`, `expenses`) and one section's fetch (`close-rates`, needing `sales_analytics`) come back 403. Today that most likely renders as the same "empty" or stuck-loading treatment as a genuine zero-data state, which is indistinguishable and misleading. This needs an explicit locked/restricted section treatment.
- **Read-only Client view.** Structurally impossible: `page.tsx` redirects to `/requests` before rendering whenever `isPreviewingClient` is true, so there is no client-preview variant to design.
- **Member seat versus admin seat.** Same page shell for both; the difference is which sections error out (see above) and, once access scoping is fixed per section 4, which rows appear inside the Finance sections that remain visible. No banner today tells a scoped member "you're seeing a restricted subset."
- **375px.** Not verified live. The page composes wide multi-column KPI strips, six recharts chart types, and native HTML `<table>` markup with fixed pixel/rem widths (several tables set an explicit `minWidth`, e.g. `32rem`) in at least ten places (`reports-content.tsx:325, 808, 1072, 1515, 1808, 2105, 2344, 2788, 2917, 3313`) with no visible responsive fallback, card, or horizontal-scroll affordance called out in the code read. Treat 375px as unverified and high risk: this is the densest untested page in the group.
- **768px.** Not verified live; same risk as 375px, somewhat reduced.
- **Dark mode.** Styling in `reports-content.tsx` is built from `var(--color-*)` token references throughout (no hardcoded hex spotted in the sampled sections), so it should inherit dark mode correctly by construction, but this has not been eyeballed with `.dark` applied. The Recharts colour source (`@/lib/chart-colors`) is shared with the live Pipeline board and should already be dark-aware; unverified for this specific page.
- **Print or public.** Not applicable. This is an internal operational tool with no client-facing, shareable, or printable variant.
- **Fixed Costs inline edit/add in flight.** Not traced in detail; treat as needing the standard inline-save pattern (per-row saving indicator, error banner on failure) consistent with the rest of the app rather than a bespoke one.
- **CSV export (Response Time section only).** Instant client-side download, no loading state, no server round trip, no confirmation toast traced.

## 4. Features and actions

### What works today
- All ten sections render live data from their respective API routes, each independently fetched via `useSWR`, each respecting the shared `displayCurrency` / `exchangeRates` conversion so every money figure on the page reformats together when the currency selector changes.
- Per-route feature gating is real and correctly split at the API layer even though the front end does not reflect it: `overview`, `response-time`, `retainer-health`, `utilization`, `worklog` gate on `reports`; `sales`, `financial-trends`, `cash-flow-forecast`, `invoice-aging`, `bank-balances`, `client-profitability`, `expenses` gate on `financial_reports`; `close-rates` gates on `sales_analytics`.
- `retainer-health`, `client-profitability`, and `worklog` correctly call org-level access scoping (`getOrgScope` / `resolveAccessScoping`) so a scoped hire's data is restricted to their assigned clients for those three routes specifically.
- A full CRUD commitments editor (Fixed Costs section): add, edit, and deactivate a fixed cost with cadence (monthly/quarterly/annual/one-off), category, currency, and an optional Xero account link, computing a monthly-equivalent figure that feeds the Cash Flow forecast.
- Two manual Xero sync actions on the Xero P&L section ("Sync P&L", "Sync Bank Balances"), each a real `POST` to `/api/admin/integrations/xero/sync-pnl` / `/sync-balances`, with a disabled-while-in-flight state and an inline result message before the next scheduled `cron/sync-xero` run overwrites the same data anyway.
- CSV export of the Response Time table, generated client-side from the already-fetched rows.
- The sticky jump nav (`SectionTabs`) with grouped labels (Operations, Sales, Finance, Team) and an embedded currency switcher.

### What exists but is wrong or half built
- **The whole page is a scheduled deletion, not a page to polish.** CT.19 (4d, TASKS.md line 184, catalogue line 65): six sections duplicate `/sales-analytics` (the Pipeline parent section plus its five sub-sections), five sections duplicate `/financial-reports` (Financial Health, Fixed Costs, Cash Flow, Xero P&L, Client Margin), and the Fixed Costs editor here has already diverged from the one on `/financial-reports` writing to the same endpoint. Batch I in the catalogue's proposed order (I1) frames this explicitly as "triage by deletion," not a redesign of ten sections into a nicer ten sections.
- **No access-scoping helper is called on eight of the eleven API routes behind this page** (`overview`, `response-time`, `sales`, `close-rates`, `financial-trends`, `cash-flow-forecast`, `bank-balances`, `expenses`, `utilization`, plus `invoice-aging` which is not wired into this page's UI at all today despite existing as a route). A scoped hire holding `financial_reports` or `sales_analytics` sees studio-wide numbers, not their own clients'. This is a real permission leak, not a cosmetic gap.
- **The front end never reads the caller's resolved feature access**, so it cannot hide the Finance or Sales jump-nav groups (or their sections) from a member who holds `reports` but not `financial_reports`/`sales_analytics`. Those sections' fetches 403 silently and render as an unlabelled empty or stuck state, which is what the catalogue means by "three feature gates hide behind one static list": the page has one static `REPORTS_SECTIONS` array with no relationship at all to the three actual permission resources (`reports`, `financial_reports`, `sales_analytics`) gating the routes underneath it.
- **A fourth, undeclared feature gate sits inside the Xero P&L section.** The "Sync P&L" and "Sync Bank Balances" buttons are rendered unconditionally for anyone who can see the section at all (gated only on `financial_reports` at the read level), but the write routes underneath them (`/api/admin/integrations/xero/sync-pnl`, `/api/admin/integrations/xero/sync-balances`) additionally require `settings.integrations`. A team member holding `financial_reports` but not `settings.integrations` sees two live-looking buttons that will always 403 with "Sync failed. Check Xero connection.", a misleading message that blames the Xero connection for what is actually a permission gap.
- **No `EmptyState` component anywhere on the page**; empty and error states are ad hoc inline text or, in several sections, entirely unhandled.
- **No `DataTable` anywhere on the page**; every tabular section (at least ten of them) is a raw hand-rolled `<table>`/`<thead>`, several with a hardcoded `minWidth` that has no verified mobile fallback.
- **`PageHeader` is used only in the loaded state**, not the loading state, matching the catalogue's "Partial (PageHeader only)" note; the loading state is a bespoke `<h1>`.

### What is planned or missing
- **CT.19, triage by deletion (4d).** Delete the six Pipeline sections (they belong on `/sales-analytics`, itself getting the same functionality per its own catalogue row and T707-T715), delete the five Finance sections (they belong on `/financial-reports`), and resolve the second diverged commitments editor in favour of one authoritative editor. What remains after deletion is presumably Overview, Response Time, Retainer Health, and Utilisation, an "operations and team" page distinct from the two finance/sales pages, but the exact post-triage shape is Liam's call; see section 7.
- **The access-scoping helper CT.19 names as owed** ("plus the access scoping it never calls," catalogue line 144): every surviving route needs `getOrgScope`/`resolveAccessScoping` wired in, matching the pattern already correct on `retainer-health`, `client-profitability`, and `worklog`.
- **T707-T715, Intelligence and analytics** (BankRunwayCard statement rows, outstanding KPI dedup T708, revenue per head, client LTV, pipeline quality scoring): named against `/financial-reports` and `/sales-analytics` as the destination surfaces, not `/reports`, reinforcing that this page is not where new analytics work should land.
- **T571, Deal-to-Client LTV link:** folds into T711-T713, same destination note as above.
- Design proposal: an explicit "restricted" section treatment (locked icon, one line of copy, no chart) for a scoped member whose feature grant does not cover a given section, replacing the current silent 403-into-empty behaviour. Marked "proposal": the backlog names the *scoping bug* but does not specify a UI treatment for a partially-restricted page.
- Design proposal: a distinct error state per section (retry affordance) rather than the fallback-to-empty behaviour most sections appear to have. Marked "proposal" for the same reason.

## 5. Data and integrations

- **Tables:** `organisations`, `requests`, `invoices` + `invoiceItems`, `timeEntries`, `subscriptions`, `deals` (via the pipeline math libs), `discovery_calls`/activities (response time), `commitments`, `exchangeRates`, `teamMembers`, `financial_snapshots`, `xero_pnl_snapshots`, `airwallex_balances`.
- **API routes (all under `app/api/admin/reports/`, plus `app/api/admin/commitments` and, for the two Xero sync buttons, `app/api/admin/integrations/xero/sync-pnl` and `/sync-balances`):** `overview`, `response-time`, `sales`, `close-rates`, `retainer-health`, `financial-trends`, `cash-flow-forecast`, `invoice-aging` (route exists, not currently rendered on this page), `bank-balances` (route exists, not currently rendered on this page), `client-profitability`, `expenses` (+ `expenses/category`), `utilization`, `worklog`. Every route calls `getRequestAuth` + `isTahiAdmin` first, then `requireFeature` with the resource named in section 4. The two Xero sync routes gate on `settings.integrations` instead of any `reports`-family resource, the mismatch flagged above.
- **Third parties:** Xero (P&L and category data feeding the Xero P&L and Cash Flow sections, and the `xero_pnl_snapshots` table), Airwallex (bank balance data feeding `financial_snapshots` and any bank-balance figures reused here), exchange-rate provider behind `exchangeRates`/`/api/admin/exchange-rates`.
- **Honesty requirements:** every number on this page must come from a live query, none of the ten sections is a stub or a placeholder today (unlike e.g. `/affiliates` or `/reviews` elsewhere in the catalogue), so the design pass should preserve "real data only" rather than introduce any illustrative or fixture numbers. The one dishonesty risk is structural, not numeric: sections a scoped viewer cannot actually load must not silently present as "zero" or "empty" when the true reason is a permission denial, and the diverged commitments editor must not let a studio user believe they edited the authoritative fixed-costs list when they in fact wrote to a second, drifting copy.

## 6. Design system contract

- **No Claude Design file exists for this surface at all.** The design-review checklist explicitly lists Reports among the "Not designed yet" surfaces (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-13-design-review-checklist.md:48`), and a full listing of the Claude Design project (57bf60cf-5e6d-450f-9e2f-e25c8d12fd66, checked 2026-09-13) confirms no `reports*.jsx`/`.css` file, no reports section inside `ops.jsx`, and no reference anywhere. This design pass starts from a blank page, not a critique of an existing draft.
- **What the live code gets right that a design should keep:** `PageHeader` (once loaded), the shared `SectionTabs` sticky-jump-nav composite (already used and standardised for this exact "long page, many anchors" pattern), `KPIStrip`/`KPICell` for the top-line numbers, `SkeletonChart`/`SkeletonTable`/`SkeletonProgressList` as the app's real skeleton primitives, `useDisplayCurrency` for a page-wide currency switch, and `lib/chart-colors` for chart colour consistency with the live Pipeline board.
- **What the design must change, because CT.19 makes most of the current page moot:** do not design ten sections. Design should assume the post-triage shape is a smaller "operations and team" page (Overview KPIs, Response Time, Retainer Health, Utilisation, roughly) once Pipeline sections move conceptually to `/sales-analytics` and Finance sections to `/financial-reports`. Since Liam has not yet ruled on the exact post-triage section list (see section 7), the safest brief is: design the primitives and the page shell (header, jump nav, KPI strip, one canonical empty/error/restricted-section treatment) in a way that scales down cleanly to four sections or holds at ten if Liam decides against deletion.
- **Primitives to specify that the live page currently lacks:** an `EmptyState` treatment for a section with genuinely zero data; a distinct error/retry treatment per section; a "restricted, ask an admin" treatment for a section a scoped viewer's grant does not cover; a `DataTable`-based (or otherwise responsive, non-`minWidth`-pinned) tabular pattern for every table currently hand-rolled with `<table>`; a mobile behaviour for dense multi-column tables and multi-series charts at 375px, since none of the ten sections has one today.
- **Leaf radius and tokens:** apply the standard leaf-radius/CSS-token rules from CLAUDE.md to any new icon backgrounds, avatar wrappers, or primary CTAs introduced (e.g. a "restricted section" icon badge); the page has no existing leaf-radius usage to preserve or break.
- **No critic verdict exists to honour or override** (no SHIP/FIX/REDO on record for this surface), unlike most other studio pages in the catalogue.

## 8. Acceptance for the design review

1. At 1440px light and dark, the page opens with `PageHeader` (title + subtitle) visible immediately, not only after data loads, no raw, unstyled `<h1>` flash.
2. The sticky jump nav is visible and legible in both themes, with the currency selector reachable without obscuring any jump-nav label.
3. Every section that could plausibly be empty (a fresh workspace with few clients, no commitments, no time entries) has a designed empty state with an icon, a title, and one line of guidance, not blank whitespace or a bare sentence.
4. At least one section demonstrates a designed "restricted, ask an admin" treatment distinct from its empty and loaded states, proving the reviewer that a scoped member's partial access has an honest visual answer.
5. Every table in the design collapses to a legible pattern at 375px with no horizontal scroll of the page itself (a deliberately scrollable table region is acceptable if visually indicated; the page shell must not scroll sideways).
6. Every touch target in the design (jump-nav items, the currency selector, any Fixed Costs row action) measures at least 44px tall at 375px.
7. Dark mode at 1440 and 375 shows no washed-out or invisible chart series, KPI value, or table row against its background, and no hardcoded light-only surface.
8. The design's section list is traceable to a specific decision in section 7 (either the current ten sections with a stopgap label, or the smaller post-triage set) rather than silently picking one without surfacing the open question.
9. No section in the design implies functionality the backend does not have today (for example, no polished CSV export button on a section other than Response Time, which is the only one wired to a real export).
10. The two Xero P&L sync buttons ("Sync P&L", "Sync Bank Balances") either surface a distinct "you don't have access to sync integrations" state for a viewer who lacks `settings.integrations`, or are hidden from that viewer entirely, rather than remaining clickable and failing with a message that blames the Xero connection.
