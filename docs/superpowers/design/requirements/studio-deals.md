# Design requirements: studio-deals

Group: studio-deals (audience: studio only). Routes: `/deals` (kanban, list; "forecast" is a header KPI, not a third view); `/deals/[id]`.
Prepared 2026-09-13 for the Claude Design pass. Source: CLAUDE.md, STATUS.md, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-13-page-catalogue.md`, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-13-design-review-checklist.md`, TASKS.md, live code, and `sales-pipeline.jsx` (the `Deals` and `DealDetail` exports) in the Claude Design project (57bf60cf-5e6d-450f-9e2f-e25c8d12fd66).

## 1. Purpose and audiences

`/deals` and `/deals/[id]` are the studio's sales pipeline: the working record of every opportunity from first contact to won or lost, and the one page Liam checks daily for what the business is worth right now. It answers "what is in play, what is it worth, and what needs a push."

Audiences:
- **Liam and Staci (super admin / owner).** Full pipeline regardless of `teamMemberAccess` scoping.
- **A scoped Tahi team member** granted the `deals` feature. Sees only deals whose org falls inside their `teamMemberAccess` scope, PLUS every unlinked deal (`orgId = null`). `app/api/admin/deals/_access.ts` documents the rule explicitly: an unassigned pipeline opportunity belongs to no client tenant, so showing it cannot leak one client's data to another, and hiding it would break the board for a scoped PM since every deal starts unlinked. A caller scoped to nothing sees nothing, including unlinked deals.
- **Nobody else.** Studio-only, no client variant. `lib/feature-tree.ts` registers `deals` with `appliesTo: ['team']` only. `app/(dashboard)/deals/page.tsx` and `[id]/page.tsx` both redirect to `/overview` unless `isAdmin` is true, and also redirect when `isPreviewingClient` is true (the admin-as-client preview cookie), with an explicit comment: a Client-view preview must never let one client's pipeline data leak into a simulated client session.
- A sub-feature gate exists inside Deals: **`deals.engagement_health`** (`Gate feature="deals.engagement_health"`) controls only the `EngagementHealthCard` on the detail page (a live delivery rollup across the deal's schedules), not the page itself.

What it must never show:
- A client organisation, ever, to a scoped member outside their access. Enforced on every route in the tree (`requireDealAccess` / `denyIfDealOrgOutOfScope` in `app/api/admin/deals/_access.ts`) for GET, PATCH, DELETE, and the activities, calls, contacts, convert-to-client and nudges sub-routes.
- Client-facing money framing. This is an internal working number (upfront + monthly x a forecast horizon, weighted by stage), not an invoice or a client-visible figure; nothing on this surface should read as something a client could see.
- A nudge recipient the delivery gate actually withheld, presented as if it were sent. See section 4: this was a real historical bug (a list of three prospects and one tahi.studio address delivered to the one, but the deal timeline named all four) and is now fixed; any redesign must not reintroduce it.

## 2. Pages, sub pages and entry points

- **`/deals`** (list/board). Reached from the sidebar "Sales" group (Leads, Calls, Deals, Proposals, Schedules, Contracts, Calculator, Sales analytics, Affiliates), and by `?new=1&orgId=<id>` (e.g. from a client detail page) which opens the New Deal dialog pre-filled to that org. Server-gated by `requirePageFeature('deals')` after the admin/preview checks.
  - **Board view** (`view=kanban` local pref, key `pipeline.viewMode`, `useUserPreference`, browser-local not account-wide): one column per open `pipelineStages` row plus Won/Lost columns whenever a deal occupies them, drag-and-drop cards between columns.
  - **List view** (`view=list`): a sortable table, sort key persisted the same way (`pipeline.sortKey`).
  - **New Deal dialog** (`showNewDeal` local state, not a route): title, company search, owner, stage, source, upfront-or-range value with currency, monthly retainer, expected close date.
  - **Deal Close dialog** (`pendingClose`/`closing` local state): opens automatically when a card or the detail's Stage card is moved to a Won or Lost stage. Won asks for a won-source; Lost asks for a structured lost-reason plus a free-text note.
  - **Filter panel** (`showFilters` toggle, not a rail, see section 6): Owner, Source, Min/Max value.
- **`/deals/[id]`** (cockpit). Reached by opening a card or list row from `/deals`, from a client detail page's Money tab (Deals sub-tab), from a promoted lead, and from deal-linked notifications. No sub-routes; every panel is either always-rendered in the two-column layout or a modal/dialog over it:
  - **Always-rendered main-column panels** (not previously listed here): `DiscoveryCallsCard` (discovery/kickoff/scope calls, own CRUD, refreshes the activity timeline via `onChanged`), the Activity Timeline card itself, `NotesSection` (inline-editable notes field), and **`AssociatedRequests`** (comment-tagged `T306` in `deal-detail.tsx`), a live "Associated Requests" card that fetches `/api/admin/requests?orgId=...` for the deal's linked org, with its own loading skeleton and empty state. This card is not tracked in TASKS.md and section 6 previously described its design-file counterpart ("Requests from this company") as design-only; it is already live, the same stale-tracking pattern as the Client LTV card in section 4.
  - **Activity Form dialog** (`showActivityForm`): Log a call/meeting/email/note/task against the deal.
  - **Nudge dialog** (`showNudgeDialog`): compose and send-now or schedule-for-later an email to linked contacts.
  - **Stage selector** (inline card, not a dialog): a dropdown/stepper to move the deal to any stage; moving to a closed stage routes through the same Close dialog as the board.
  - **Value breakdown editor** (inline, `editing` state in `ValueBreakdown`): edit upfront/monthly/currency/dates without leaving the page.
  - **Engagement editor** (inline, `EngagementEditor`): project vs retainer type, total hours or hours/month, start/end dates, feeding the "Capacity Impact" read-out.
  - **Contact linker** (`ContactLinker`, opens a `Popover` search): link/unlink contacts to the deal.
  - **Org selector, Owner selector, Source selector** (inline pickers, each its own small component).
  - **Convert to Client card**: appears only once the deal's stage is closed-won; one click provisions/activates the client organisation (see section 5).
  - **DealSalesKit** (`components/tahi/deal-sales-kit.tsx`, shared with the client-detail hub): one-click create-and-open Proposal / Schedule / Contract prefilled from the deal.
  - **Archive confirm** (inline two-step, `confirmingArchive`): soft-deletes the deal (`closeReason = 'archived'`) via `DELETE /api/admin/deals/[id]`; hidden once already archived.

## 3. States and variants

- **Loading, `/deals`.** `LoadingSkeleton()` renders while the merged SWR fetch (stages + deals in one key) is in flight. One shape for both board and list.
- **Loading, `/deals/[id]`.** A `SkeletonCard` + `SkeletonList` for the main column, one `SkeletonCard` for the side column, in the same two-column grid the loaded page uses.
- **Empty, no deals at all.** Board: each column renders "No deals" inline (dashed border, no icon, no CTA, a much quieter empty than the list). List: a full centred empty card, leaf-radius icon tile, "No deals yet" / "Create your first deal to get started", no CTA button rendered (the header's "New Deal" button is the only entry point). These two empty treatments disagree with each other and with the design file's single shared `EmptyState` (icon, title, body, a "New deal" CTA button, and a secondary "Go to leads" link), see section 6.
- **Empty, filtered to zero.** Not explicitly modelled: filtering to zero rows on `/deals` renders the same board/list empty states above (column "No deals" / list's full "No deals yet" card) rather than a "no deals match your filters" message with a clear-filters action. This is a real gap against the design file, which has a distinct inline `EmptyState` ("No deals match" / "Clear filters").
- **Error.** Not modelled as a distinct state anywhere in the tree. A failed SWR fetch on `/deals` falls back to `pipelineData?.deals ?? []`, i.e. renders as "no deals" rather than an error with a retry action. `/deals/[id]` has a `!deal` branch ("Deal not found") that covers a 404 but not a genuine fetch failure.
- **Read-only Client view.** Structurally impossible on either route: both pages redirect to `/overview` before rendering whenever `isPreviewingClient` is true. There is no client-preview variant to design.
- **Member seat versus admin seat.** Same UI, fewer rows for a scoped member (their orgs plus unlinked deals only); no banner tells a scoped member their pipeline is partial, unlike the count-aware framing elsewhere in the app.
- **375px / 768px.** Not verified live for either route. The catalogue's Pass 3 critic verdict on the design file (section 6) already flags "board lost a column, titles clip", exactly the risk area at narrow widths, and the live page has never been screenshotted at these breakpoints per STATUS.md.
- **Dark mode.** Not verified live for either route. `deals-content.tsx` and `deal-detail.tsx` both route styling through `var(--color-*)` tokens, so dark mode should be free by construction, but has not been eyeballed with `.dark` applied.
- **Print or public.** Not applicable; wholly internal.
- **Stalled deal.** Live: Stalled is its own pipeline stage (`slug: 'stalled'`, `isClosedLost: 1` in the seed data), so a stalled deal leaves the open pipeline, the weighted forecast, and every open-stage column entirely, the same as a genuinely Lost deal, it is visually distinguished from Lost only by a small Bell/BellOff icon on the card and an "Auto Nudges: Active/Paused" toggle in the sidebar. This is the exact problem MC.6 exists to fix; see section 4.
- **Drag over a column, `/deals` board.** Live: a dashed-border highlight on `dragOver`; drop calls `handleDrop`, which routes a drop onto a closed-stage column through the Close dialog rather than silently closing the deal.
- **Deal close in flight.** `DealCloseDialog` disables its submit button and shows a spinner state (`submitting`) while the PATCH is in flight; the board/detail optimistically do not move the card until the dialog resolves.
- **Nudge send in flight.** The Nudge dialog disables its send button (`sending`) while the request round-trips; on suppression (the email allowlist withheld one or more recipients) the caller learns which addresses were actually delivered versus suppressed, and the deal timeline names only the delivered set (see section 4).
- **Archive confirm.** Two-step inline (click "Archive deal" reveals a confirm/cancel pair) rather than a modal, to prevent an accidental single click from removing a deal from the pipeline.

## 4. Features and actions

### What works today
- Kanban board and list view of every open, won and lost deal (subject to org scoping), with a browser-local view and sort preference.
- Weighted forecast math (`lib/pipeline-math.ts`, `calculatePipelineTotals`) shared with `/reports` and the overview KPIs per Decision #040, so the numbers agree everywhere: Pipeline MRR, Upfront, Total Pipeline (upfront + monthly x horizon), Weighted Forecast (x stage probability), Open Deals count, all shown as header KPI cells.
- Per-stage historical win-rate probability (`historicalProbability`, `dealsSampled`), computed from how deals have actually converted through each stage, with a studio-settable default probability per stage (`/settings`, `pipeline-stages.tsx` section) as the fallback when there isn't enough history yet.
- Search (title + company), and filters for Owner, Source, Min/Max value, all client-side over the fetched set.
- Drag-and-drop stage moves on the board; moving into a closed (won/lost) stage always routes through a confirmation dialog that captures a won-source or a structured lost-reason plus a free-text note (`lib/pipeline-math`-adjacent enums, not free text alone), and stamps `closedAt`.
- Deal detail cockpit: a stage-progress stepper, an editable value breakdown (upfront/monthly/range/currency/recurring-start/engagement-end) with a sparkline-based estimate trendline built from real activity history, an editable "Next action" (one label, one due date, surfaces as a due chip on the board card), an engagement editor (project vs retainer, total hours or hours/month, feeding a "Capacity Impact" read-out), owner/source/company pickers, a contact linker, a notes field, and a "Days in stage" read-out.
- **Client Lifetime Value card**: shown on the deal detail whenever the linked org has any LTV (`ltv.total > 0`), combining won-deal value and paid-invoice total. This directly satisfies TASKS.md T571 ("Deal-to-Client LTV link") and the catalogue's "Deal to client LTV link" functionality row, **both are stale**: the feature is already live in `app/api/admin/deals/[id]/route.ts` (the `ltv` computation) and rendered in `deal-detail.tsx`. Do not re-scope this as new work.
- **Sales kit, one-click**: `DealSalesKit` creates-and-opens a real Proposal, Schedule or Contract prefilled from the deal (company, contact, title). Confirmed live per STATUS.md ("every traced button hits a real API").
- **Convert to client, one-click**: appears once a deal is closed-won; provisions or reactivates the linked organisation (reuses an existing org if the deal already has one, or one found through a linked contact; otherwise creates a new org named from the deal title), dispatches a domain event, and is gated by `PLAN_TYPE_ERROR`/`isRetainerPlanType` validation on the engagement's plan type.
- Activity timeline (call/meeting/email/note/task/stage-change/value-change/owner-change/etc, `parseActivityMetadata`) with delete-with-undo-style confirm per row.
- Send-now nudges go out for real through the one email delivery door (`lib/email-delivery.ts`), append the studio's configured signature (`pipeline.nudgeSignatureHtml` setting), and log only the addresses actually delivered (not the full recipient list) to the deal's activity timeline, a real fix for a real historical bug (see section 1).
- Auto-nudges pause/resume toggle per deal (`autoNudgesDisabled`), visible in the sidebar and as a Bell/BellOff glyph on the board card, gated to deals in the Stalled stage.
- Whole-deal archive (`closeReason = 'archived'`, soft-delete, two-step confirm), permanently excluded from the default `/deals` list query.
- MCP parity: `list_deals`, `get_deal`, `create_deal`, `update_deal`, `delete_deal`, `list_deal_activities`, `list_deal_contacts`, `add_deal_contact`, `remove_deal_contact`, `list_deal_calls`, `schedule_deal_call`, `list_deal_nudges`, `update_pipeline_stages`, `get_pipeline_stages`.

### What exists but is wrong or half built
- **Stalled is a stage, not a flag (MC.6, open).** Today a "Stalled" deal is parked in its own `pipelineStages` row with `isClosedLost: 1`: it disappears from the open pipeline, the weighted forecast, and every real stage column, and is functionally indistinguishable from a genuinely Lost deal in every KPI except a small bell icon. The design file's model (section 6) is a boolean/record flag orthogonal to stage, so a stalled deal stays visibly in its real stage (dimmed), keeps contributing to the weighted forecast, and carries a "reach out again on X" date plus a note. This is a real product-behaviour gap, not a cosmetic one: it changes what the forecast KPI numbers mean.
- **Scheduled nudges are recorded, never sent (T3.8, open).** `POST /api/admin/deals/[id]/nudges` with `sendNow: false` and a `scheduledAt` inserts a `dealNudges` row with `status: 'scheduled'` and stops; no cron, worker, or dispatcher anywhere in the codebase (`app/api/cron/*`, `workers/*`) reads that status back out and sends it. The UI presents "Queue it" / "Send now" as equally real options; only one of them does anything at the scheduled time.
- **"Auto Nudges: Active" has no engine behind it (T3.8, MC.6, open).** `autoNudgesDisabled` is a real column, faithfully read and written, but nothing in the codebase ever flips a deal INTO the Stalled state automatically or sends a nudge because a deal has sat untouched, there is no automated staleness detector at all. The toggle promises an automation that does not exist; toggling it changes nothing observable except its own badge.
- **The "Enable"/"Pause" auto-nudge button always renders the same `BellOff` icon** regardless of state (`deal-detail.tsx`, the sidebar Auto Nudges card), a small existing-code inconsistency worth fixing in the same pass, not a design decision to preserve.
- **No honesty banner when the fetch is capped.** `GET /api/admin/deals` is always called with `?limit=100`; if a studio ever has more than 100 open+closed deals, the KPI totals and "Open Deals" count silently describe only the first 100, with no banner naming the cap, unlike the design file's explicit "Showing the first 100 of 128 deals. The numbers above cover the 100 loaded, not the whole pipeline" banner.
- **No archived-deals view.** The list route unconditionally excludes `closeReason = 'archived'` rows; there is no toggle anywhere on `/deals` to include them, so an archived deal is invisible rather than filterable-back-in. The design file's rail has an explicit "Include archived" view.
- **No quick-look/peek before opening the full detail.** Every row/card click on the live board and list navigates straight to `/deals/[id]`; there is no lightweight preview equivalent to the design file's `DealPeek` slide-over (value, stage, owner, next action, a "Nudge" shortcut, an "Open deal" button).
- **Filters live in a toggled panel, not a rail** (catalogue: "Legacy composition, PageHeader only"), which is the specific thing AR.5 exists to fix across every list page in the app.

### What is planned or missing
- **T3.11 / catalogue "Port"**: the whole v3 composition swap (PageHeader + KPIStrip + ad hoc filter panel to the rail/StatBand/RailLayout shape used elsewhere), a 2-3 day effort per TASKS.md, explicitly allowed to slide.
- **MC.6**: Stalled becomes a flag on any stage, not a stage, see above; this is a schema and behaviour change (likely a `stalled` JSON/record column on `deals`, not just a UI relabel), not pure styling.
- **T3.8**: deals honesty pass, remove or implement scheduled + auto nudges (Liam's call, per TASKS.md: "1d remove, 3.5-4d build, +2d with MC.6" since building the sender properly means building at least a staleness-detection cron too), no fake "sent" state without a working delivery path (already largely fixed for send-now, per section 4 above), and error surfacing in the nudge dialog on send failure.
- **T1.16 Batch B**: per-org scoping rollout for deals (STATUS/TASKS list deals as part of the still-open "Batch B" of five entities), but the live code already shows `_access.ts` guards wired into every deals route (GET/POST list, GET/PATCH/DELETE detail, activities, calls, contacts, convert-to-client, nudges). **This appears to be further along than TASKS.md/STATUS.md record**; treat the scoping mechanism as built and verify rather than re-plan it from zero, though a fresh audit against the T1.16 checklist is still worthwhile before closing the item.
- **Proposal**: an explicit error state (distinct from "no deals") for a failed fetch on `/deals` and `/deals/[id]`, matching the design file's `ErrorState` component which the live page does not use at all today.
- **Proposal**: a "filtered to zero" empty state distinct from "genuinely no deals," matching the design file's inline `EmptyState` with a Clear-filters action.
- **Proposal**: a banner naming the scoped-member's partial view ("you're seeing N of the studio's deals") so a restricted seat cannot mistake their limited pipeline for the whole one.
- **T700-T705 (post-launch backlog, "Revenue features")**: "Deal -> invoice generation, pipeline invoice indicators" is listed in TASKS.md as post-launch, undated work directly on this surface (a closed-won deal does not today generate an invoice, and no pipeline invoice indicator exists on the board/list/detail); not previously named in this document.

## 5. Data and integrations

- **Primary tables**: `deals` (title, orgId, stageId, ownerId, value/currency/valueNzd, split upfront/monthly value model, recurringStartDate, source, estimatedHoursPerWeek, engagementType/totalHours/hoursPerMonth/engagementStartDate/engagementEndDate, expectedCloseDate, closedAt, closeReason, lostReason, nextActionLabel/nextActionDueAt, notes, autoNudgesDisabled, wonSource); `pipelineStages` (name, slug, probability, position, colour, isDefault, isClosedWon, isClosedLost, no `isStalled` or flag-style column today, see MC.6); `dealContacts` (junction to `contacts`); `dealNudges` (templateId, contactEmails JSON, subject, bodyHtml, status draft/scheduled/sent/failed, scheduledAt, sentAt); `activities` (shared timeline table across deals/leads/orgs/contacts, `dealId` nullable).
- **APIs this surface depends on**: `GET/POST /api/admin/deals`, `GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/admin/deals/[id]`, `GET/POST /api/admin/deals/[id]/activities`, `DELETE` on a single activity, `GET/POST /api/admin/deals/[id]/nudges`, `POST /api/admin/deals/[id]/convert-to-client`, `GET/POST/DELETE /api/admin/deals/[id]/contacts`, `GET /api/admin/deals/[id]/calls` (and the shared `DiscoveryCallsCard`/`scheduled_calls` writers), `GET/POST /api/admin/pipeline/stages` (studio-editable stage list and probabilities, also read by `/settings`), `GET /api/admin/pipeline/capacity` (deal-driven capacity forecast consumed by `/capacity`), `GET /api/admin/team` (owner picker).
- **Third parties**: Resend, through the one delivery door `lib/email-delivery.ts` (nudges); Google Calendar indirectly via `DiscoveryCallsCard`/`discovery_calls` for deal-linked calls; Xero/Stripe indirectly once a deal converts to a client (billing setup happens on the client record, not here).
- **Downstream consumers of this data**: `/overview` (owner-home pipeline-ahead card), `/reports` (six sections the catalogue says duplicate `/sales-analytics`), `/sales-analytics`, `/capacity` (pipeline-driven capacity impact), `/clients/[id]` Money tab (a client's own Deals sub-view).
- **Must be honest**: the weighted-forecast and pipeline-total KPIs must never silently describe a capped subset (see section 4's 100-deal cap gap); "Auto Nudges: Active" and a "scheduled" nudge status must not claim a delivery path that does not exist (see T3.8); the LTV card must only ever show real invoice/deal totals, never a placeholder; the "Convert to client" action must never silently no-op, it either provisions/reactivates a real organisation or surfaces a real error.
- **Access scoping**: `scopedOrgIds` (`lib/access-scope.ts`) plus the deals-specific `denyIfDealOrgOutOfScope`/`requireDealAccess` helpers (`app/api/admin/deals/_access.ts`), applying the "unlinked deal visible to any scope, linked deal follows its org's scope" rule documented in that file's own header comment.
- **Audit**: deal mutations write to the shared `activities` table (stage_change, value_change, owner_change, org_change, source_change, close_date_change, notes_change, won, lost, archived, nudge_sent, contact_added/removed, engagement_change), which is both the audit trail and the user-facing timeline on the detail page, the same rows serve both purposes.

## 6. Design system contract

**Primitives the live pages already use** (any redesign should keep or deliberately supersede, not silently drop): `PageHeader` (title + subtitle + one primary action), `KPIStrip`/`KPICell` (five-cell header band), `ViewToggle` (board/list), `Pagination`/`usePagination` (verified wired: `deals-content.tsx` paginates the sorted list view at a page size of 10, board view is unpaginated), `SidebarSection`/`SidebarCard` (the detail's right column), `EngagementHealthCard`, `DiscoveryCallsCard`, `DealSalesKit`, `Gate` (feature-flag wrapper). Styling already routes through CSS var tokens on both pages, so dark mode should be free by construction if a redesign keeps doing the same.

**What the existing design file (`sales-pipeline.jsx`, the `Deals` and `DealDetail` exports) gets right, that the live pages do not have today:**
- A proper left `Rail` (`RailLayout`/`RailSec`/`RailItem`/`RailSelect`/`RailInput`/`RailDir`, collapsible, with an active-filter count and a single "Clear" action) replacing the live toggled filter panel, this is exactly AR.5's rule ("filters in a left rail on every list page").
- A `StatBand` with Total pipeline, Weighted forecast (with a "X% of pipeline" sub-line), Pipeline MRR, and Open deals (with an inline "N stalled" sub-count), richer framing of the same numbers the live `KPIStrip` already computes correctly.
- **Stalled modelled as a flag, not a stage** (`D.isStalled(d)`, a `StallDialog` that records a since-date, a reach-out-again date from a fixed set of offsets, and a free-text note, all without touching `stageId`). This is the reference implementation MC.6 should build toward.
- A capped-results banner ("Showing the first 100 of 128 deals...") the live page has no equivalent of.
- An "Include archived" rail view and an "Active vs Stalled" saved-view split, neither of which the live page offers.
- `DealPeek`, a lightweight `SlideOver` quick-look (value, stage, owner, source, expected close, last touched, contact count, next action, a Nudge shortcut and an Open-deal button) reached from a row's "Quick look" menu item or overflow icon, before committing to the full detail page.
- On `DealDetail`: a horizontal stage-progress rail with per-stage odds percentages and Won/Lost/Stalled actions living directly on the Stage card; a Value card with reason chips ("Why did it change?") captured alongside every value edit, plus an estimate-history sparkline; a "Calls" card with an inline Schedule action; a "Requests from this company" card; a "Sales kit" card (proposals/schedules/contracts, both existing links and one-click create) matching the live `DealSalesKit`; a "Delivery health" card (health.overall on_track/at_risk, phases done, named off-track items) matching the live `EngagementHealthCard`; a "Danger zone" card with an inline archive confirm matching the live archive flow; explicit empty-state copy on every sub-card ("No calls on this deal. Schedule one and the pre-call digest goes out half an hour before," "A deal without a contact cannot be nudged," "Work raised for this company shows up here once the deal is won"). **Correction**: the last of these, "Requests from this company," is not design-only as first drafted here, it is already live as `AssociatedRequests` (see section 2); the redesign should keep it, not introduce it.
- Confirms the "sales kit one-click create" and "convert to client" flows are meant to keep working exactly as they do live; the design file does not regress either.

**What the design must change before it can be trusted as the target:**
- **Critic verdict is FIX, not SHIP** (catalogue, Pass 3): "board lost a column, titles clip, two accent rules." Liam's AR.3 ("Leads, Calls and Deals prototypes approved by Liam as-is") is a separate, earlier signoff and should not be read as overriding the Pass 3 critic finding, the two must be reconciled, not silently merged, before this ports.
- **The design's Stalled-as-a-flag model has no corresponding schema today.** Before this can port, MC.6 needs a real data model decision (a `stalled` JSON/record column on `deals`, or a small side table), this is backend work the design pass should assume is coming, not itself solve, but the interaction design (StallDialog fields: since, reach-out-again date, note) is a solid, ready-to-build reference.
- **The design's NudgeDialog offers "Send now" / "Queue it" as if both are equally real**, matching the live UI's same honesty gap (T3.8). Any redesign pass should either build the scheduled-send engine alongside it or visibly demote/remove the "Queue it" option, not simply re-skin the existing two-button choice.
- **No design coverage of a genuine error/fetch-failure state** distinct from "no deals" is confirmed present (`ErrorState` is imported and used, unlike live), good, but the corresponding live-code work (section 4) has to land for this to be true end to end.
- **No design coverage of the org-scoped "partial pipeline" banner** proposed in section 4; neither the design file nor the live code names this state today.

- The left rail (filters, saved views, sort) matches the AR.5 shape used elsewhere in the app, not the flat toggled panel the live page uses today.
- Every state in section 3 is present and visually distinguishable at both breakpoints: loading, empty-no-deals, empty-filtered-to-zero, and an explicit error state.
- If Stalled-as-a-flag (MC.6) is in scope for this pass, a stalled deal visibly stays in its real stage (dimmed or otherwise marked) rather than disappearing into its own column, and the reach-out-again date and note are visible without opening the detail page.
- The deal detail cockpit shows the Sales kit, Delivery health, Contacts, LTV (when present) and Danger zone cards without visually implying any of them work differently than they do live (no new dead buttons).
- Dark mode (`.dark` class applied) shows no contrast regressions on stage pills, money figures, the stalled-state dimming treatment, and every dialog (Close, Stall, Nudge, New Deal).
- Every touch target on the redesigned board, list, and detail is at least 44px tall at 375px, matching CLAUDE.md's mobile rule; the board in particular must have a usable mobile fallback (the design file's own list view already degrades to mobile cards, confirm the board does too, or confirm list is the enforced default at 375).
- If the "Queue it" scheduled-nudge option or the "Auto Nudges" toggle remain in the redesign, their copy does not claim automated sending unless section 7's decision was to build the engine.
- No placeholder number anywhere on the page (KPI band, deal cards, LTV card) reads as real when it is a stub or a capped/partial figure.
